Walk to one corner of the space you want to use, mark it, walk to the opposite corner, mark that. You get a box nobody can leave.

Players inside an arena are moved into their own routing bucket. They stop existing to the rest of the server, which is what keeps gunfire from ever reaching a dispatch script — nothing to patch, nothing that breaks when your dispatch resource updates.
